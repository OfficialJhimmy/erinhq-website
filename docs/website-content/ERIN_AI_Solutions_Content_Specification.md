## 1. Strategic Role of the AI Solutions Section
AI Solutions is the commercial centre of the ERIN website. It should help a business visitor recognise a problem they have, understand the type of system that could solve it, see how the system would fit into their existing organisation, and start a conversation.
These are not presented as generic software products that anyone can buy off the shelf. They are productised solution concepts that ERIN can design, customise and build around the organisation's existing workflow, tools, data and requirements.

## 5. Why Some Original Names Were Changed
Shortlet Booking Website has been reframed as a Shortlet Booking & Guest Concierge System because a plain booking website is primarily a software project. Adding guest communication, automated enquiries, recommendations, booking support and follow-up makes it a stronger AI solution.
E-commerce Store with AI Product Descriptions has been reframed as an AI-Powered E-commerce Store because the commercial value is broader than generating descriptions. The system can combine commerce with AI-assisted catalogue creation, search, recommendations and customer assistance.
Restaurant Menu & Ordering Website has been reframed as a Restaurant Ordering & Customer Experience System. A basic menu website is not an AI solution. The stronger product combines digital ordering with customer support, recommendations, upselling and operational notifications.
WhatsApp AI Business Assistant has been added because conversational business automation is highly relevant to businesses that already use WhatsApp as a customer and sales channel.

## AI Solutions: UX & Implementation Rules
### Catalogue Cards
- Cards should feel like solution products, not blog posts.
- Each card should clearly state the business problem or audience.
- Use concise copy with strong hierarchy.
- Do not overwhelm the catalogue with technical terminology.
- Each card must lead to a dedicated solution page.
- Use filters by business function or industry rather than only technical category.
### Individual Solution Page Layout
1. Breadcrumb
1. Hero
1. Business problem
1. What the solution is
1. How it works
1. Features
1. How it fits into the organisation
1. Benefits
1. Integrations
1. Human oversight
1. Who it is for
1. Customisation
1. Implementation approach
1. Final CTA
1. Related solutions
### Visual Language
- Use product UI mockups, workflow diagrams and system architecture visuals.
- Avoid generic robot illustrations and stock AI imagery.
- Where possible, show a realistic example of the system interacting with a business tool.
- Use subtle animation to explain workflows, not decoration.
- Keep the visual language consistent across all solution pages.
- Use real screenshots when a solution has already been built or prototyped.
### SEO Strategy
Each solution page should target a distinct search intent. Do not create multiple pages that compete for the same exact keyword.
- AI customer support agent
- AI lead qualification agent
- AI knowledge assistant
- AI recruitment screening system
- AI research automation
- AI document intelligence
- AI contract intelligence
- AI shortlet booking system
- AI e-commerce store
- Instagram AI chatbot / DM automation
- AI for law firms
- AI healthcare workflow automation
- Real estate AI lead qualification
- AI logistics tracking assistant
- WhatsApp AI chatbot for business
### Global + Nigeria SEO
The solution pages should be globally positioned. Do not put 'Nigeria' in every page title. Instead, establish ERIN as 'Based in Lagos, Nigeria. Working globally.' across the site.
Create separate location-focused landing pages only when there is enough useful, original content to justify them. Examples could include /ai-engineer-nigeria, /ai-automation-nigeria and /ai-engineer-lagos. These should not be duplicated versions of the solution pages.
### Conversion Rules
- Every solution page must have a clear CTA above the fold and at the bottom.
- CTA language should invite a business conversation, not imply instant checkout.
- The enquiry form should ask what the organisation is trying to achieve, current workflow, existing tools, expected users and preferred contact details.
- Allow visitors to select the solution they are enquiring about automatically from the CTA.
- Include a 'Something else?' option so custom enquiries are not blocked.
### Important Content Guardrails
- Do not claim a solution has been deployed for a client unless it has actually been deployed.
- Separate 'built', 'prototyped', 'available to build' and 'concept' clearly.
- Do not fabricate ROI numbers or performance claims.
- Do not imply that AI replaces professional judgement in legal, healthcare, finance or other high-stakes contexts.
- Use human approval and escalation language for sensitive workflows.
- Keep the copy practical, specific and commercially understandable.
- Use British English.
- Never start a sentence with 'And'.
- Avoid em dashes and en dashes throughout the website.
### Recommended CTA Variations
- Build This for My Business
- Discuss This Solution
- See How This Could Work for Us
- Talk About My Workflow
- Build a Custom Version
- I Have a Different Problem
## Final Commercial Principle
The AI Solutions section should answer five questions before asking a visitor to contact ERIN:
1. What problem does this solve?
1. What does the system actually do?
1. How does it work?
1. How would it fit into my organisation?
1. What would my organisation gain from having it?
The visitor should then have a simple next step: start a conversation about building the solution around their organisation.
